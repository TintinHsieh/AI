# Campus Customs design direction

Campus Customs now treats the storefront like a small editorial lookbook rather than a generic catalogue.

- **Typography and palette:** DM Sans carries readable shopping copy, DM Mono supplies the New Haven field-note labels, and Playfair Display gives headlines a collegiate-magazine contrast. The ink-black, Yale-blue, pink, and warm-cream palette keeps product images prominent while making calls to action easy to spot.
- **Hierarchy:** The home hero uses an oversized italic blue moment, a rotating CC seal, orbit lines, lookbook captions, and a kinetic ticker to establish the brand before the catalogue. The Products page now opens with a New Haven edit banner and a clear collection-to-controls-to-cards sequence.
- **Product presentation:** Cards use a pink scanline on hover/focus, a gentle image lift, saved-piece control, price, stock signal, and an Open / piece editorial cue. This makes browsing feel tactile while keeping purchase-relevant facts visible.
- **Motion and accessibility:** Hero copy, cards, orbit marks, and chat affordances enter with restrained motion; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the animation-heavy layer for shoppers who request less motion.
- **Chat feel:** The assistant has a CC avatar, account-aware status line, note-style response kicker, animated thinking dots, capsule suggestions, a send-mark input, and a pulsing launcher. It reads like a helpful shop desk rather than a detached utility window.

These choices create visual rhythm and small moments of discovery that encourage shoppers to browse another card, save a piece, or ask the assistant a follow-up question without obscuring price, stock, or navigation.
