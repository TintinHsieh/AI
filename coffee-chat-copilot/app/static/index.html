How it works

  1. Reach out after an event. The app offers three 90-minute windows that fit your classes and the train, and holds them.
  2. Intake agent reads the email: who, which bank, in person or virtual, their availability.
  3. Scheduler agent searches your calendar, Metro-North trains, and NYC travel times for three 90-minute windows.
  4. Validator re-checks every option against your classes and other holds. The AI cannot invent a time.
  5. Responder agent writes the reply in your finance club format. A checker blocks leaks and wrong times.
  6. The three times are held so no other banker gets them, until one is confirmed.
