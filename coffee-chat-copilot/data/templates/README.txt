Finance club email templates (from the club's Outlook .oft files).
One file per situation. First line "Subject: ..." is optional (replies keep "Re:").
Lines starting with # are rules for the Responder agent and are never sent.
Placeholders are filled by the app:
  {banker_first_name} {banker_name} {bank} {event} {referrer} {options} {confirmed_time}
  {meeting_details} {location} {when} {specifics} {new_banker} {my_name} {signature}
